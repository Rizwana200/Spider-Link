# 🕷️ Spider-Link

> An intelligent campus lost-and-found platform designed for colleges with multiple blocks, rooms, laboratories, and common areas.

## 📌 Overview

Spider-Link is a full-stack lost-and-found platform designed to help students and staff recover lost belongings across large college campuses.

In colleges with multiple academic blocks, classrooms, laboratories, libraries, hostels, and common areas, finding a lost item can be difficult. An item may be lost in one location and found in another, making manual identification time-consuming.

Spider-Link provides a centralized platform where users can report lost and found items and discover potential matches using information such as **category, color, description, location, time, and image-related information**.

The platform uses an explainable matching workflow called **Spider Sense** to identify potentially related lost and found reports.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Large college campuses can contain:

- Multiple academic blocks
- Hundreds of classrooms
- Laboratories
- Libraries
- Seminar halls
- Hostels
- Canteens
- Parking areas
- Other common spaces

When students lose their belongings, they often depend on:

- Asking classmates
- WhatsApp groups
- Checking different blocks
- Contacting college authorities
- Manually searching through lost-and-found notices

These approaches make it difficult to efficiently connect a lost item with its corresponding found item.

Spider-Link aims to provide a centralized and structured solution for this problem.

---
